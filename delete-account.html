Delete your The Trader account

The Trader · published by MVL Sai Krishna

Delete inside the app

  1. Open The Trader and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Can't open the app?

Email mvlsaikrishna@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or include the mobile number you signed in with. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your sign-in record (mobile number or email), user ID and profile. Purchase records may be kept for up to 8 years where Indian tax law requires it. Progress stored on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings.
