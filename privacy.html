Privacy Policy

The Trader · Last updated 15 October 2026

The Trader ("the app") is a stock-market education app published by MVL Sai Krishna, Hyderabad, India. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: mvlsaikrishna@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number or your email address, used to create your account and sign you in. If you sign in with email, your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
  • Account identifier: a random user ID created by Firebase when you sign up.
  • Subscription records: which sections you have subscribed to and until when, plus the purchase token or transaction ID from Google Play or the App Store. We use these only to unlock the content you paid for. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details — payments are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple.
  • Last sign-in time, to keep your account working and to help with support.

Lesson progress, quiz answers, streaks and paper-trading results are stored only on your device.

The app does not use advertising, does not use an advertising ID, does not track your location and does not access your contacts, photos or files.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To check your subscriptions with Google Play or Apple and unlock paid sections.
  • To answer support requests you send us.

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone for marketing.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database and server functions, hosted in Mumbai, India) to run the app, and Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store to process subscriptions. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. Information is encrypted in transit.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. When you delete your account we delete your sign-in record and profile within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept for up to 8 years where Indian tax law requires it. Cancelling a subscription is done separately in Google Play or the App Store.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Education only

The app provides education about financial markets. It does not provide investment advice, stock tips or trading signals, and it does not show live market prices.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top.
